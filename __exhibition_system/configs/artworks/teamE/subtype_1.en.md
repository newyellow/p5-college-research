With the opening of the Hsuehshan Tunnel in 2006, the Yilan outside the train window began to transform. Convenient transportation brought flows of people and waves of tourism; the cityscape became denser, and buildings gradually stretched upward. Rice fields remain, but B&B signs and tourist facilities have started to appear beside the ridges, and housing prices fluctuate accordingly. The window view of this era presents a tug-of-war between tradition and modernity: Guishan Island still watches from afar, but the nearby towns grow increasingly busy. The train speed increases, and the scenery outside flows faster, symbolizing Yilan's entry into a new stage of development. Through this window, we see how convenience and change descend upon this land simultaneously.
