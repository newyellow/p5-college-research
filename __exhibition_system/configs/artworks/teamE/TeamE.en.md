With "Window Views" as its core concept, this work constructs a journey through time via train window frames from three different eras: a 1960s train, a post-2006 train after the opening of the Hsuehshan Tunnel, and a hypothetical high-speed rail in 2036. Looking at Yilan through windows of different eras, the life scenes composed of fields, mountains, coastlines, fishing boats, ducks, urban buildings, and fluctuating housing prices make the scenery a witness to the changing times. The three works not only present landscapes but also capture the rhythm, sense of life, and unique temperature of Yilan in each era. Through the transformation of window frames, we are invited to reflect: What has changed, and what has remained in the Yilan we see across different times?
