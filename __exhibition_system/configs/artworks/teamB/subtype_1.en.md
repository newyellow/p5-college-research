This piece chooses the Vase with Dragon-Shaped Handles and Deer Motif as an element. Inspired by the leaping deer on the vase, it brings to mind the ducks strolling in various postures across the Lanyang Plain at different times. After passing through the tunnel amidst white clouds, one enters the Lanyang Plain, greeted by a landscape composed of mountains, the sea, and Guishan Island, where many ducks swim leisurely. Feeling hungry? Have some Yilan delicacies! Although a bit heart-wrenching, the delicious taste of duck meat is indeed a feast for the taste buds.
