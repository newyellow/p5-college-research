Based on Yilan's geography—surrounded by mountains on three sides and facing the sea on one—this work integrates the recording spirit of the Qing dynasty official document Gazetteer of Kavalan Subprefecture into generative art. Most impressions of Lanyang stop at its landscapes, but it is only through the participation of "people" that the land gains a name and sublimates into culture.

As an official ancient record, the Gazetteer of Kavalan Subprefecture detailed the transformation of this land from its natural state to social regulation. This work views the mountains and sea as the stage and "people" as the protagonists. Through generative algorithms, artifacts like the Jade Brush Rest in the Shape of a Mountain Range and Bone-Carved Human Figurine Dagger Hilt are transformed into visual codes, recreating the human pulse recorded in ancient texts within randomly interwoven images. Through the trinity of "Mountain, Sea, and People," we see not only geographical landscapes but also the footprints of civilization—humanity coexisting with nature and dialoguing with history across the river of time.
