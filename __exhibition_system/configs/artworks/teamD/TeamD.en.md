Yilan is an all-encompassing place. This work combines objects and photos representing Yilan with artifacts, reconstructing and representing them through the theme of a kaleidoscope. Multi-faceted mirrors dismantle and reorganize images, refracting fragments of Yilan's landscape and life within a rotating order. Viewers can interact via an interface to instantly change the image structure and rhythm within the kaleidoscope, turning viewing into operation and perspective into narrative. In the Chinese title, "Lan" points to Yilan, "Eye" symbolizes viewing and focusing, and "Jing" (Crystal) refers to the light-splitting texture of multi-faceted mirrors. Between each rotation and setting, it presents how Yilan is recombined and seen anew.
